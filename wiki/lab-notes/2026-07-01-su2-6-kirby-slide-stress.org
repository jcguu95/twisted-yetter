* Evaluation Formula

對一個 knot =K=，simple label =j=，framing =f=：

#+begin_src text
RT_j(K, f) = d_j * J_{j+1}(K; q=A^4) * theta_j^f
#+end_src

其中：

- =J_{j+1}= 是 Sage 的 reduced colored Jones polynomial；
- =A=zeta32=；
- =q=A^4=；
- =theta_j = A^{j(j+2)}=；
- =d_j= 是 =SU(2)_6= quantum dimension。

這個 normalization 用既有 =SU(2)_2= right trefoil convention 校準：

#+begin_src text
J_2(right trefoil; zeta16^4) = -1
d_1 * J_2 = -sqrt(2)
#+end_src

這和舊 =SU(2)_2= oracle 的 fixed-label trefoil value 相符。

* Kirby Slide Model

Before:

#+begin_src text
K colored by Omega_a
split framed unknots U_r colored by Omega_{b_r}
#+end_src

After sliding =K= over all =U_r=：

#+begin_src text
K framing changes by sum(fr(U_r))
U_r becomes a meridian of K
Omega label on K changes from a to a + sum b_r mod 2
Omega labels on U_r stay b_r
#+end_src

固定標號下，meridian component =i= around knot label =j= 作用為：

#+begin_src text
S_{i,j} / S_{0,j}
#+end_src

這正是前一輪 Hopf-star / fusion-channel gate 的具體 Kirby-slide 用法。

* Tested Slide Scenarios

七種 slide-over framed unlink scenarios：

- slide over one =+1=-framed unknot
- slide over one =-1=-framed unknot
- slide over two =+1=-framed unknots
- slide over one =+1= and one =-1= framed unknot
- slide over three mixed framed unknots =(1,1,-1)=
- slide over four mixed framed unknots =(1,-1,1,-1)=
- slide over five mixed framed unknots =(1,1,-1,1,-1)=

對每個 case，所有 =Omega= labels 都測：

#+begin_src text

= 8 * 136

其中 unique colored-Jones braid inputs 是 6 個；framed variants 會重用同一份
colored Jones root values，只改 framing factor。
