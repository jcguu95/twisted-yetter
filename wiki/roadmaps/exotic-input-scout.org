* Ranking criteria

資料可用性排序採用以下保守標準：

1. closed smooth 4-manifold pair already targeted by =twisted-yetter.org=；
2. known or claimed explicit handlebody / Kirby diagrams in the literature；
3. chance of no 1- and no 3-handles, so 2-handle components can plausibly serve as an
   initial =H_2= basis after verification；
4. marking source, preferably cork-canonical rather than ad hoc basis matching；
5. digitization effort: final diagrams, component lineage, framings, dotted status,
   orientation, and basis-change matrices.

* Dolgachev assessment

Working assessment: \(E(1)_{2,3}\) vs \(E(1)\) remains the first candidate ranked by input
availability.

Reasons:

- =twisted-yetter.org= already selects it as the first closed exotic pair.
- The plan records Akbulut's Dolgachev-surface handlebody as an explicit no-1/no-3-handle
  route and as a cork twist of \(E(1)\). This must be treated as a source-backed claim to
  verify, not as repo-local data.
- If no 1- and no 3-handles are verified, the 2-handle linking matrix can become the first
  machine-checkable presentation for \(H_2\), intersection form, signature, Euler
  characteristic, and decoration coordinates.
- A cork decomposition is the most plausible route to an auditable \(H_2\) comparison:
  first at the =cork-canonical distinction= level, not immediately at the unmarked
  total-function level.

Current blockers before n70:

- =SOURCE NEEDED=: capture the Akbulut paper metadata, PDF/hash or arXiv locator, and exact
  figure/page numbers for the final \(E(1)\) and \(E(1)_{2,3}\) diagrams.
- Digitize both final diagrams into machine-readable component data: PD code or braid word,
  component IDs, framings, orientation, dotted status, and handle lineage.
- Verify the no-1/no-3-handle claim from the source and record any implicit handles or
  normalization consequences.
- Compute and record linking matrices, ranks, determinant/parity checks, signature, Euler
  characteristic, and expected intersection form.
- Reconstruct the cork-complement basis and the integer basis-change matrices from the
  source diagrams to the final diagram components.

Conclusion: Dolgachev passes n65 as the first ranked candidate by input availability, but it
does not pass n70 and should not be used for invariant computation yet.
