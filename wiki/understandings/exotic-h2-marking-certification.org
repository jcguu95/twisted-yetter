* Comparison-Level Vocabulary

The ledger fixes the future comparison-level vocabulary:

- =marked=: a specific \(H_2\) identification is part of the claim.
- =cork-canonical=: a cork twist/action supplies the canonical comparison data.
- =orbit= / =multiset=: the comparison is made after an explicitly recorded
  automorphism action or multiset construction.
- =unmarked=: the full total function is compared under the allowed
  homeomorphism/isometry action.
- =inconclusive=: current source data do not support any comparison claim.

The current ledger uses =comparison_level=inconclusive= with status
=inconclusive-source-blocked=.  The other labels are schema language only until
the required matrices, sources, and lineage exist.

* Required Certification Fields

Future certification must record:

- comparison level: =marked=, =cork-canonical=, =orbit=, =multiset=,
  =unmarked=, or explicitly =inconclusive=;
- two side \(H_2\) bases, including basis ids, basis vectors, coordinate
  convention, and source locators;
- exact integer intersection matrices in the recorded bases;
- homeomorphism / basis identification matrix, or a source-backed reason for
  absence;
- component-to-\(H_2\) lineage through every slide, cancellation, and basis
  change;
- cork twist marking/action data if =cork-canonical= is used;
- source citations with =ref:= ids plus page/theorem/figure/crop locators;
- sanity checks: rank agreement, exact integer/symmetric matrices,
  form preservation, determinant/signature/Euler checks, alpha=0 ordinary route,
  and unimodularity of basis-change matrices.
