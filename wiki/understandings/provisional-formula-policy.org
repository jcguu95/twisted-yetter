This node is a policy/gating node.  It is not a proof of a mathematical formula, not a
source audit of Yetter or Crane-Kauffman-Yetter, and not an implementation of a twisted
formula.

The purpose is to allow downstream agents to compute many provisional examples without
silently upgrading those computations into closed 4-manifold invariants or exotic
smooth-structure claims.

* Rejected Claims

The policy rejects:

- =claim-supporting closed 4D invariant=
- =decorated/twisted formula theorem=
- =exotic detection claim=
- =Reutter escape/no-go conclusion=

Passing many examples may become computed evidence for a candidate formula.  It is not proof of
decorated Kirby invariance, not a closed invariant certificate, and not exotic detection.

Likewise, negative examples or equal values do not imply a Reutter-style no-go theorem.  They
route to source/formula/code triage and, if reproducible, to a later obstruction audit.
