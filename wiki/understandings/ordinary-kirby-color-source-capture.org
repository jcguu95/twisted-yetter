* Boundary From n18

=n18-colored-framed-link-evaluator-gate= has passed at commit =2cf77a5= with =pytest=: 220
passed and CTL verify status ok.  It accepts the restricted bare =SU(2)_2= colored framed link
evaluator core as a deterministic exact calculator layer for the supported machine-readable link
fixtures.

The boundary exposed to =n22= is the evaluator entry point
=evaluate_su2_level2_link(LinkDiagram, labels_by_component)= and the gate contract recorded in
=wiki/understandings/colored-framed-link-evaluator-gate.org=.  Ordinary Kirby-color code must call
that accepted core for supported colored sectors; it must not re-use fixture expected values, matrix
statistics, or a separate shortcut evaluator.

Accepted n18 scope available as upstream machinery:

- empty link, split unlink, framed unknot, and positive Hopf controls through the same restricted
  evaluator entry point;
- exact =QuadraticSurd= / =RootOfUnityPower= values serialized as =coefficient_times_twist=;
- structured blockers for unsupported general crossing diagrams and dotted components;
- claim level =computed evidence/restricted-evaluator-core-gate=.

This does not imply any of the following:

- ordinary Kirby-color formula;
- closed 4-manifold invariant;
- 4D surgery normalization;
- signature anomaly correction;
- handle-slide, blow-up/down, 1/2 cancellation, or 2/3 cancellation invariance;
- ordinary Crane-Yetter / Yetter theorem-level value;
- exotic smooth-structure distinction.

For =n22=, n18 only supplies a link-control baseline that Kirby-color experiments may call after
they label their outputs as local-convention / experimental.  For =n24=, n18 is necessary but far
from sufficient.

* Precise Source Slots Required Before Claim-Level Use

Every slot below is currently =SOURCE NEEDED=.  The point of this node is to make the missing source
labels explicit enough that later agents can fill them without guessing.

** Kirby color coefficients

Needed source data:

- exact definition of the ordinary Kirby color in the selected source;
- coefficient notation, e.g. whether the source writes \(d_i\), \(\Delta_i\), quantum trace
  dimension, bracket loop value, or another symbol;
- exact index set over simple objects / labels and whether dual labels are identified;
- signs, root-of-unity specialization, and any parity convention;
- relation, if any, between the link-evaluator unknot value and the Kirby-color coefficient;
- whether the source assumes a modular, ribbon, spherical, tortile, or other category structure.

Working target only:

\[
\omega \sim \sum_i d_i X_i
\]

This schematic target is not a sourced theorem in this repo.  In particular, do not identify
\(\Delta_i=d_i\) at theorem level until the source record gives page / definition labels and the
RT/Kauffman convention is reconciled with the surgery source.

** Global dimension and normalization

Needed source data:

- source definition of global dimension / total quantum dimension / \(N\), \(\mathcal D\), or
  equivalent notation;
- whether the invariant uses \(\sum_i d_i^2\), its square root, an inverse power, or a signed /
  root-of-unity variant;
- normalization exponent in terms of handle counts, number of link components, nullity, rank,
  Euler characteristic, or other presentation data;
- whether the empty Kirby diagram for \(S^4\) is normalized to \(1\), a global constant, or a
  category-dependent value;
- how unresolved global factors behave under comparing different presentations.

Claim boundary:

An experimental =n22= implementation may store a field named
=experimental_global_dimension_convention=, but no output using that field may support a closed
4-manifold invariant claim until this slot is sourced.

For the current toy convention, =sum_i d_i^2 = 4= may be recorded only as a local
=SU(2)_2= sanity check.  It is not yet the sourced global normalization for a closed 4D surgery
formula.

** Signature anomaly / framing convention

Needed source data:

- sign convention for positive link framing and its relation to the intersection form diagonal;
- convention for positive / negative eigenvalues and \(\sigma(Q)\);
- whether the surgery formula uses \(\sigma(Q)\), \((b_+,b_-)\), nullity, Gauss sums, or a
  category anomaly;
- effect of changing every framing sign / orientation reversal;
- blow-up/down correction or equivalent Kirby I move normalization;
- compatibility with the RT/Kauffman framed-unknot twist convention selected for n18.

Claim boundary:

It is unsafe to compare values for \(CP^2\) and \(\overline{CP^2}\), or to treat a blow-up/down
move as normalized, until this slot is page-level sourced or proved in repo.

The accepted link evaluator has a framed-unknot twist convention for colored link evaluation.  A
future ordinary surgery layer still needs a source crosswalk from that twist convention to the
signature anomaly / central charge factor, if the selected formula uses one.

** Handle-slide invariance proof scope

Needed source data:

- exact theorem / proposition showing handle-slide invariance for the ordinary Kirby color;
- hypotheses on the input link, category, orientation, and framing;
- whether the proof is for 3D RT surgery, 4D Broda / Crane-Yetter surgery, Yetter's twisted
  surgery expression, or a restricted presentation;
- exact algebraic identity used by the Kirby color under sliding;
- whether the proof treats only 2-handle slides or also dotted-circle slides / 1-handle slides.

Claim boundary:

Passing n18 Hopf and unlink controls does not prove handle-slide invariance.  A future =n22= test
may include slide toy examples as computed evidence, but =n24= needs the proof scope pinned.

** 1/2 cancellation treatment

Needed source data:

- whether the surgery formula supports Kirby diagrams with dotted 1-handles;
- how a cancelling 1/2 pair is represented in the source's link or handle notation;
- normalization factor that makes adding/removing such a pair harmless;
- relation between dotted-circle conventions and the closed state-sum invariant;
- whether simply connected no-1-handle presentations are an explicit hypothesis or only a local
  simplification.

Claim boundary:

If the first ordinary implementation excludes dotted components, its claim must say
=2-handle framed-link experiment= or =restricted no-1-handle presentation=, not general closed
4-manifold invariant.

** 2/3 cancellation treatment

Needed source data:

- how 3-handles are encoded or suppressed in the surgery formula;
- normalization factor depending on \(b_1\), \(b_2\), \(b_3\), Euler characteristic, link
  nullity, or handle count;
- exact condition under which a 2-handlebody calculation closes to a closed 4-manifold value;
- whether implicit 3-/4-handles are unique or require extra presentation metadata;
- compatibility with \(S^4\), \(CP^2\), and \(S^2\times S^2\) ordinary controls.

Claim boundary:

This is the main barrier between a 2-handlebody invariant/statistic and a closed 4D invariant.  Do
not let a no-dotted framed link calculation silently cross this boundary.

** Closed 4-manifold versus 2-handlebody distinction

Needed source data:

- exact statement of the closed ordinary invariant being computed;
- exact theorem connecting the link/surgery expression to the closed invariant;
- source hypotheses on connectedness, orientability, category semisimplicity, handle
  decompositions, and triangulations;
- whether boundary 3-manifold or 2-handlebody data appears in the construction;
- what data must be included in a certificate to distinguish presentation-level output from
  closed-manifold output.

Claim boundary:

Until this slot is filled, outputs should use claim levels like
=experimental/local-convention=, =computed evidence=, or =2-handlebody-level only=, never
=claim-supporting closed 4-manifold invariant=.

* Provisional Contract For n22

=n22-ordinary-kirby-color-implementation= may be useful before all source slots are filled, but
only under the following contract.

** Fields safe as experimental / local convention

The implementation may define and serialize:

- =kirby_color_convention=: e.g. =experimental-ordinary-omega-v0=;
- =kirby_color_coefficients=: exact local coefficients for the chosen toy category;
- =link_evaluator_gate_source=: pointer to n18 certificate / source boundary;
- =global_dimension_convention=: =experimental-global-dimension-v0= or =SOURCE NEEDED=;
- =signature_correction_convention=: =experimental-signature-correction-v0= or =SOURCE NEEDED=;
- =handle_scope=: e.g. =no-dotted-2-handle-framed-link-only=;
- =claim_level=: =experimental/local-convention=;
- =source_status_by_slot=: map whose keys include all slots in this ledger;
- =normalization_status=: =SOURCE NEEDED= unless a later source record fills it;
- exact arithmetic values and deterministic JSON serialization;
- fixture paths, diagram ids, labels, framings, linking matrix sanity data, and source-record ids.

These fields can support regression tests, debugging, and ordinary-control fixtures.

** Fields that must not support mathematical claims yet

The following may be present only as warning / TODO / source-status fields:

- theorem-level Kirby color coefficient identification;
- theorem-level global normalization;
- theorem-level signature anomaly correction;
- handle-slide invariance proof;
- 1/2 or 2/3 cancellation invariance;
- blow-up/down invariance;
- equality with ordinary Crane-Yetter / Yetter closed invariant;
- comparison across different handle presentations as a presentation-independent value.

** Required refusal behavior

n22 should refuse or clearly mark unsupported cases:

- dotted components unless the 1/2 cancellation source slot is filled;
- non-toy general crossing links unless the link evaluator genuinely supports them;
- diagrams whose handle scope exceeds the serialized =handle_scope=;
- requests for =closed_4_manifold_claim=true= while any source slot remains =SOURCE NEEDED=;
- comparisons where unresolved normalization factors differ.

** Acceptance checklist for the next medium worker

To accept =n22-ordinary-kirby-color-implementation= as an experimental/local-convention scaffold,
the next worker should verify all of the following without upgrading any =SOURCE NEEDED= slot:

- every sector term is generated by a transparent finite coloring expansion over ordinary
  components;
- every supported sector calls =evaluate_su2_level2_link(LinkDiagram, labels_by_component)= from
  the accepted n18 core;
- sector records include labels, coefficient product, evaluator value, exact contribution, and
  deterministic ordering;
- the output records =kirby_color_convention=, =handle_scope=, =claim_level=,
  =normalization_status=, and =source_status_by_slot=;
- =closed_4_manifold_claim=True= is refused while any source slot remains =SOURCE NEEDED=;
- dotted components and diagrams outside the restricted evaluator scope produce structured
  blockers rather than guessed values;
- empty diagram, 0-framed unknot, +1-framed unknot, and split unlink controls have exact regression
  expectations stated as local convention only;
- Hopf / \(S^2 \times S^2\) ordinary controls are either supported through the accepted evaluator
  or explicitly blocked with the unresolved source/implementation reason;
- no ordinary surgery normalization, signature correction, handle cancellation theorem, twisted
  formula, decorated formula, exotic input, or general crossing evaluator is implemented as part of
  n22.
