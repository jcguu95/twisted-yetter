* First Formula Target

** Working target

The first computational target is a minimal exact Kirby-matrix / =omega/theta= formula for small
closed examples, initially in the =A = Z/2= setting described in =ref:project-plan-twisted-yetter=.

Working notation:

\[
F_{\mathcal C,X}(\alpha)=Z_{\mathcal C}(X,\alpha),
\qquad
\alpha\in H_2(X;A),
\qquad
A=\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\operatorname{Id}_{\mathcal C}).
\]

Output domain:

\[
F_{\mathcal C,X}:H_2(X;A)\to k
\]

where =k= is the category's ground field / coefficient field. The exact field conventions are
=SOURCE NEEDED= from Yetter / Crane-Kauffman-Yetter before certificate-level computations.

** Inputs for the closed state-sum invariant

- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: a closed oriented 4-manifold =X=.
- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: category data =\mathcal C= sufficient for the Yetter / Crane-Yetter
  state sum. The project plan phrases this as finite semisimple, spherical, ribbon/premodular
  or tortile-type category data, but the exact original assumptions are =SOURCE NEEDED=.
- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: a decoration =\alpha \in H_2(X;A)=, equivalently via Poincare duality
  as an =H^2(X;A)= class when the stated hypotheses allow that identification.
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: exact cycle/cochain representative convention in Yetter's state sum.
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: exact normalization constants and whether the invariant is normalized to
  ordinary Crane-Yetter, Broda, or another convention.

** Inputs for the first Kirby-matrix toy formula

- =ASSUMPTION=: the Kirby presentation is a closed simply connected 4-manifold described by a
  framed link with no dotted 1-handles and with the implicit 3-/4-handles controlled by the
  source formula.
- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: the matrix input is the integral linking/framing matrix =Q= of the
  2-handles, together with a decoration vector representing =\alpha= in the 2-handle basis.
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: exact hypotheses in Yetter's surgical formula, including whether it requires
  simply connectedness, absence of 1-handles, and how 3-handles are encoded.
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: exact orientation convention for the framed link and the sign convention for
  the intersection form / signature.

* Category-Side Ledger

** Groups and characters

- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: use =U(\mathcal C)= for the universal grading group.
- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: use
  \[
  A=\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\operatorname{Id}_{\mathcal C})
  \]
  for tensor automorphisms of the identity functor.
- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING=: for a simple object =X_i= and =a\in A=, Schur's lemma gives
  \(a_{X_i}=\lambda_i(a)\operatorname{id}_{X_i}\).
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: Yetter's exact notation for the grading group and Proposition 4 identifying
  tensor automorphisms with characters of the grading group.

** Ordinary and twisted Kirby colors

- =WORKING UNDERSTANDING= for =A=Z/2= case from project plan:
  \[
  \omega=\sum_i\Delta_i\, i,\qquad
  \theta=\sum_i(-1)^i\Delta_i\, i.
  \]
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: whether =\Delta_i= equals the quantum dimension =d_i= in the source convention,
  or differs by a sign / dual / root-of-unity convention.
- =SOURCE NEEDED=: exact index set and parity convention for =(-1)^i=.
- =ASSUMPTION=: in first toy code, =\omega= is the undecorated / trivial-sector color and
  =\theta= is the nontrivial =Z/2= decorated color.
- =CONJECTURAL UNTIL PROVED=: for general finite abelian =A=,
  \[
  \Omega_a=\sum_{i\in\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)} d_i\,\lambda_i(a)\,X_i.
  \]
  This formula is not a theorem in this repo.
