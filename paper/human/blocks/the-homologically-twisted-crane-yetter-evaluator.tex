\section{Homologically Twisted Crane--Yetter Theory}
\label{sec:evaluator}
\label{sec:twisted-evaluator}

We begin with the untwisted premodular handle evaluator: framed components are
summed over simple labels and dotted components insert the tensor-unit
projector.  The homological twist makes one change only.  A handle cocycle
supplies the exponent of a character coupon on each framed component.  We
first isolate this modification, then show that every untwisted local proof
survives after checking naturality, monoidality, duality, and tensor-unit
absorption for those coupons.  Marked Kirby--Cerf completeness finally
descends the presentation-level value to an \(H_2\)-marked 4-manifold.
