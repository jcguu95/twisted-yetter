\section{Homologically Twisted Crane--Yetter Theory}
\label{sec:evaluator}
\label{sec:twisted-evaluator}

We define the evaluator directly from a one-ended marked Kirby datum: framed
components are summed over simple labels, dotted components insert the
tensor-unit projector, and the homology marking supplies the exponents of the
character coupons.  We then prove independence of the cocycle representative
and invariance under the nine moves in a one-ended connected Cerf movie.
Marked Kirby--Cerf completeness finally shows that the value is well defined
on an \(H_2\)-marked 4-manifold.
