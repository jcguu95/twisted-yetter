\section{The homologically twisted Crane--Yetter evaluator}
\label{sec:evaluator}
\label{sec:twisted-evaluator}

We first collect the categorical and graphical ingredients of the formula,
then define its value on a marked Kirby datum.  We next prove independence of
the cocycle representative and invariance under every marked Kirby--Cerf
move.
Marked Kirby--Cerf completeness then shows that the value is well defined on
an \(H_2\)-marked four-manifold.
