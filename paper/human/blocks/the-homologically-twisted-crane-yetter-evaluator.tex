\section{Homologically Twisted Crane--Yetter Theory}
\label{sec:evaluator}
\label{sec:twisted-evaluator}

We first define the evaluator for an arbitrary marked Kirby datum by composing
the elementary handle operators.  We then derive its explicit dotted--framed
link formula after reducing to one \(0\)-handle and one \(4\)-handle.  We next
prove independence of the cocycle representative, the two extreme-handle
cancellation identities, and invariance under the nine moves in a one-ended
connected Cerf movie.  Marked Kirby--Cerf completeness then shows that the
value is well defined on an \(H_2\)-marked 4-manifold.
