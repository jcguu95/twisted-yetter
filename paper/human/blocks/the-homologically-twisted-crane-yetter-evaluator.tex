\section{The homologically twisted Crane--Yetter evaluator}
\label{sec:evaluator}
\label{sec:twisted-evaluator}

The construction is a direct decoration of marked Kirby data.  Framed
\(2\)-handle components receive Kirby colours and character coupons; dotted
\(1\)-handle components apply the tensor-unit projector to the ribbons passing
through the handle.  The resulting finite sum of closed ribbon graphs is
evaluated by the ordinary RT graphical calculus.
