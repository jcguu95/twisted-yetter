\begin{remark}[Comparison with the untwisted Crane--Yetter theory]
\label{rem:untwisted-comparison}
Here ``untwisted'' means that the character is the identity monoidal natural
automorphism; it does not mean that the ribbon twist of \(\cC\) is trivial.
When \(\eta=\operatorname{id}\), every character coupon in
\eqref{eq:twisted-handle-evaluator} is an identity, independently of the
marking.  The remaining label sum, tensor-unit projectors, and normalization
agree with the closed skein handle formula of
\cite[Definition~5.11]{Tham2021}: reversing a primal handle decomposition
interchanges indices \(k\) and \(4-k\), so primal \(1\)-handles give its
index-\(3\) unit-channel projection, primal \(2\)-handles give its Kirby-colour
sum, and primal \(3\)-handles give the inverse global-dimension factors.
The convention comparison is recorded in
\cite[Example~9.1 and Remark~9.2]{Tham2021}.  The equivalence with the PLCW
and original state-sum formulations is
\cite[Theorems~5.25, 5.26, and~3.61]{Tham2021}.  Consequently
\[
  \CY_{\cC,\operatorname{id}}(X,\alpha)
  =Z^{\mathrm{sk}}_{\mathrm{CY},\cC}(X)
  =Z_{\mathrm{CY},\cC}(X),
\]
and this value is independent of \(\alpha\).
\end{remark}
