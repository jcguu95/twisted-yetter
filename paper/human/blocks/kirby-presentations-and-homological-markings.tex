\section{Marked Kirby presentations}
\label{sec:kirby-presentations}

This section performs the first arrow in the construction:
\[
  (X,\alpha)\longmapsto (\mathcal H,[a]).
\]
Here \(\mathcal H\) is a Kirby presentation of \(X\), while \([a]\) is the
handle-cohomology class corresponding to \(\operatorname{PD}_X(\alpha)\).
This marked handle data is the complete topological input passed to the
evaluator in \cref{sec:twisted-evaluator}.
