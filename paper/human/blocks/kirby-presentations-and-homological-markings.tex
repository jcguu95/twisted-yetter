\section{Marked Kirby presentations}
\label{sec:kirby-presentations}

This section performs the first arrow in the construction:
\[
  (X,\alpha)\longmapsto(\mathcal H,\xi_{\mathcal H}),
  \qquad
  \kappa_{\mathcal H}(\xi_{\mathcal H})
  =\operatorname{PD}_X(\alpha).
\]
Here \(\mathcal H\) is a special Kirby presentation of \(X\), while
\(\xi_{\mathcal H}\) is its handle-cohomology marking.  The evaluator in
\cref{sec:twisted-evaluator} is displayed using a handle cocycle
\(a\in Z^2(\mathcal H)\) representing \(\xi_{\mathcal H}\), but the resulting
value will be independent of that representative.
