\subsection{Independent numerical checks}
\label{app:numerical-checks}

The comparisons in this subsection were made with earlier versions of the
diagrammatic evaluator.  They are evidence for conventions and normalization,
not ingredients in the proof of \cref{prop:modular-formula}, and do not by
themselves certify the evaluator constructed in this paper.  Every
record cited below is a repository-relative, machine-readable artifact.

\begin{example}[$D(\mathrm{Dic}_3)$ on $\mathbb{CP}^2$]
\label{ex:dic3-cp2-numerical}
Let \(\cC=D(\mathrm{Dic}_3)\) be the untwisted Drinfeld double of the
order-$12$ dicyclic group, and let \(\eta\) be the order-$4$ character
represented by the invertible object used in our calculation.  Here
$\operatorname{Dim}(\cC)=144$, the standard
$\mathbb{CP}^2$ Gauss value is numerically $12$, and
$\theta_{g_\eta}=-1$.  Let \(e\) be the positive generator of
\(H_2(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)\).  Thus \cref{ex:projective-planes} predicts
for every \(n\in\mathbb Z\) that
\[
 \widehat{\CY}_{\cC,\eta}(\mathbb{CP}^2,ne)=12(-1)^n.
\]
The independent numerical evaluator applied to the $a=1$ specialization of
Yasui's Figure~2 \cite{Yasui2008} returned, for $n=0,1,2,3$,
\[
 12-1.04\!\times\!10^{-14}i,
 \quad -12+9.88\!\times\!10^{-15}i,
 \quad 12-1.04\!\times\!10^{-14}i,
 \quad -12+9.58\!\times\!10^{-15}i.
\]
The largest discrepancy from the corresponding standard-presentation value
was $2.13\times10^{-14}$.  These numbers are from
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure2-cp2-a1-r1-dic12-omega0-range-combined-aggregate-v1.json}.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[$D(A_4)$ on a nonseparated presentation]
\label{ex:da4-yasui-a1}
Yasui's Figure~3 with $a=1$ presents
$\mathbb{CP}^2\#2\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{\,2}$ \cite{Yasui2008}.  For the
untwisted double $D(A_4)$, the theorem predicts the same value on this
nonseparated presentation as on the split $(+1,-1,-1)$ unlink.  The complete
$14^3=2744$-sector computation produced raw value
\[
 1727.9999999999975-2.21\times10^{-11}i
\]
and normalized value
\[
 12.0000000000000-1.54\times10^{-13}i.
\]
The split raw prediction was
\[
 1.72800000000000\times10^3-3.07\times10^{-12}i.
\]

For the cocycle-twisted category $D^{\omega}(A_4)$ denoted \texttt{c3w1},
putting the generator character on the component corresponding to the first
exceptional class leaves the predicted phase equal to $1$.  The same
$2744$-sector evaluator returned normalized value
$12.000000000000483-1.60\times10^{-13}i$.  The two records are respectively
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure3-a1-r1-normalized-da4-omega0-untwisted-singleton-aggregate-check-v1.json}
and
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure3-a1-r1-normalized-da4-c3w1-twisted-component-a-singleton-aggregate-v1.json}.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[A visible $D(\mathrm{Dic}_3)$ character phase]
\label{ex:dic3-yasui-a1}
On the same Figure~3 presentation, let \(\cC=D(\mathrm{Dic}_3)\) and let
\(\eta\) be the witness character, for which
$\theta_{g_\eta}=-1$.  Under the recorded coordinate identification,
\cref{ex:diagonal-rational-surfaces} therefore predicts that the component-A
generator twist changes the normalized value from $12$ to $-12$.  The
historical evaluator returned
\[
  12.000000000000007-1.38\times10^{-14}i
  \quad\longmapsto\quad
  -12.000000000000023+9.61\times10^{-14}i.
\]
The observed ratio differed from $-1$ by approximately $6.1\times10^{-15}$;
the absolute normalized difference between twisted and untwisted values was
$24$ up to roundoff.  The legacy artifact name uses \texttt{dic12} for this
order-$12$ group; the compact trace is
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure3-a1-r1-normalized-dic12-witness-g-component-a-twist-compact-trace-v1.jsonl}.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[$SU(3)_2$ and a cubic phase]
\label{ex:su3-2-yasui-a1}
For the working AnyonWiki realization of $SU(3)_2$, the invertible generator
has twist $e^{4\pi i/3}$.  The modular formula predicts that the relevant
component-A marking multiplies the untwisted Figure~3 value by
\[
 e^{4\pi i/3}=-\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i.
\]
The artifact
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure3-a1-r1-normalized-su3-2-anyonwiki-aggregate-v1.json}
found ratio
\[
 -0.499999999999998-0.866025403784436i,
\]
with absolute ratio error $3.14\times10^{-15}$ and absolute raw-value change
$61.9371864212031$.
This check is tied to that explicitly selected $F/R$ convention; a second
unreconciled table convention is not included in the claim.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[$SU(2)_8$ and a trivial predicted phase]
\label{ex:su2-8-yasui-a1}
For $SU(2)_8$, the available simple-current character has trivial twist in
the quadratic factor relevant here.  The theorem therefore predicts no
change between the Figure~3 and split-presentation functions after the
recorded coordinate identification.  Version~3 of the all-marking comparison
checked eight marking rows, each containing $9^3=729$ fixed-label sectors;
the maximum absolute difference was
$1.3235946575869639\times10^{-12}$.  The record is
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/yasui/figure3-a1-r1-normalized-su2-8-all-alpha-raw-comparison-v3.json}.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[Akbulut--Yasui Figure~21]
\label{ex:figure21-pointed}
The manifold $E'_3$ in Akbulut--Yasui Figure~21 is homeomorphic to
$\mathbb{CP}^2\#9\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{\,2}$
\cite[Section~6, Theorem~6.1 and Figure~21]{AkbulutYasui2008}.  Consequently
for every modular twisted categorical input \((\cC,\eta)\), the theorem
predicts, for every \(\alpha\in H_2(E'_3;\mathbb Z)\),
\[
 \widehat{\CY}_{\cC,\eta}(E'_3,\alpha)
 =\tau_+(\cC)\tau_-(\cC)^9
  \theta_{g_\eta}^{-Q_{E'_3}(\alpha,\alpha)}.
\]
The retained numerical batch tested $15$ pointed modular
realizations of ranks $2$ through $5$.  After a presentation normalization
derived independently from the untwisted aggregate, all $15$ value
distributions matched the split standard presentation; $13$ matched without
correction, while the two semion rows required the same correction $-1$.
This is only a distribution-level check: the Figure~21 transcription has not
been certified against the printed diagram, and no certified pointwise
$H_2$ coordinate map was available.  The fail-closed normalization record is
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/akbulut-yasui/figure21-pointed-ordinary-normalization-audit-v1.json}.
\end{example}

As a broader implementation check, the native Morse evaluator recorded
$23$ successful cross-presentation comparisons for
$SU(2)_k$ at $k=2,3,4,5,6,8$, $SU(3)_2$, $D(A_4)$, a cocycle-twisted
$D(A_4)$, and $D(\mathrm{Dic}_3)$.  This finite matrix tests the conventions
used above but does not replace the proof of \cref{prop:modular-formula}.  Its
record is
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/morse/native-morse-figure2-cross-category-matrix-v1.json}.
