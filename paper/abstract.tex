\begin{abstract}
Let $\cC$ be a premodular fusion category over $\mathbb{C}$ and let
$\eta\colon\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}\Rightarrow\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}$ be a
monoidal natural automorphism.  From a special Kirby diagram we define a
homologically twisted Yetter invariant $\CY_{\cC,\eta}(X,\alpha)$ for a closed
oriented smooth four-manifold marked by $\alpha\in H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$, and
prove that it is invariant under marked oriented diffeomorphism.  When the
marking is trivial, the construction recovers the untwisted Crane--Yetter
invariant.

Extending Reutter's stabilization argument \cite{Reutter2023}, we show that on
simply connected manifolds the invariant is determined by the marked integral
intersection lattice.  The marked extension is not formal: unlike the
untwisted theory, it requires a stable diffeomorphism that carries the
prescribed homology class to the prescribed homology class.  Marked realization
provides this control in the simply connected case, yielding explicit odd and
even formulas and showing that the invariant does not detect exotic smooth
structures there.  The non-simply-connected marked case, for which such control
is not presently available, and the non-semisimple case remain open.
\end{abstract}
