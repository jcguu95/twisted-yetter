\begin{abstract}
Let $\cC$ be a premodular fusion category over $\mathbb{C}$ and let
$\eta\colon\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}\Rightarrow\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}$ be a
monoidal natural automorphism.  From a special Kirby diagram we construct a
fixed-character homologically twisted Crane--Yetter evaluation
$\CY_{\cC,\eta}(X,\alpha)$ for a closed connected oriented smooth
four-manifold marked by $\alpha\in H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$.  We prove the
projector, character, fusion, and normalization identities required for
Kirby invariance.  Relative Kirby calculus then proves that the evaluation is
invariant under marked oriented diffeomorphism.  When the marking is trivial,
the construction recovers the untwisted Crane--Yetter invariant.

Reutter's stabilization argument \cite{Reutter2023} then shows that on simply
connected manifolds the
evaluation is determined by the marked integral intersection lattice.  The
marked extension is not formal: unlike the
untwisted theory, it requires a stable diffeomorphism that carries the
prescribed homology class to the prescribed homology class.  Marked realization
provides this control in the simply connected case, yielding explicit odd and
even formulas and showing that the invariant does not detect exotic smooth
structures there.  The non-simply-connected marked case, for which such control
is not presently available, and the non-semisimple case remain open.
\end{abstract}
