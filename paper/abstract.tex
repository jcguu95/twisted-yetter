\begin{abstract}
Let $\cC$ be a premodular fusion category over $\mathbb{C}$ and let
$\eta\colon\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}\Rightarrow\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}$ be a
monoidal natural automorphism.  We give a Kirby-diagram formula for the
homologically twisted Yetter invariant $\CY_{\cC,\eta}(X,\alpha)$ of a closed
oriented smooth four-manifold marked by $\alpha\in H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$, and
prove its invariance under marked oriented diffeomorphism.

For simply connected manifolds, stabilization and marked realization reduce
the invariant to the marked integral intersection lattice.  We obtain explicit
odd and even formulas from standard $\mathbb{CP}^2$,
$\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^{\,2}$, $K3$, and $S^2\times S^2$ blocks, and a single
closed formula in the modular case.  Consequently this semisimple homological
refinement does not detect exotic smooth structures on simply connected closed
four-manifolds; the non-simply-connected and non-semisimple cases remain open.
\end{abstract}
