\section{Future directions}
\label{sec:future-directions}

\begin{conjecture}[Non-simply-connected detection]
\label{conj:non-simply-connected-detection}
There exist a semisimple premodular fusion category $\cC$ over $\mathbb C$,
a monoidal natural automorphism $\eta$ of its identity functor,
homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic closed oriented non-simply-connected
smooth four-manifolds $X$ and $Y$, an orientation-preserving homeomorphism
$f\colon X\to Y$, and a class $\alpha\in H_2(X;\mathbb Z)$ such that
\[
  \CY_{\cC,\eta}(X,\alpha)
  \ne\CY_{\cC,\eta}(Y,f_*\alpha).
\]
\end{conjecture}

The simply connected argument uses marked stable realization in an essential
way.  We do not assume an analogue for arbitrary fundamental groups, so the
preceding conjecture is not ruled out by
\cref{cor:no-simply-connected-exotic-detection}.

\begin{conjecture}[Full coefficient-group extension]
\label{conj:full-coefficient-extension}
Let
\[
  A_{\cC}=\operatorname{Aut}_{\otimes}(\operatorname{Id}_{\cC}).
\]
The fixed-character construction should extend to an invariant
\[
  \CY_{\cC}(X,-)\colon H_2(X;A_{\cC})\longrightarrow\mathbb C
\]
whose pullback along
\[
  H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow H_2(X;A_{\cC}),
  \qquad n\longmapsto\eta^n,
\]
is the invariant $\CY_{\cC,\eta}$ constructed in this paper.
\end{conjecture}

The coefficient group $A_{\cC}$ and its action on simple objects are those
of \cref{prop:characters-universal-grading}; this is also the coefficient
system used in \cite[Definitions~1 and~9]{Yetter1993}.  Establishing the
extension requires redoing representative independence and every
character-aware attach--transport--detach calculation with arbitrary
$A_{\cC}$-labels rather than powers of one fixed character.  In particular,
the resulting numerical formulas would require the family of twisted Gauss
sums
\[
  \tau^{\pm}(a)
  :=\sum_{i\in\operatorname{Irr}(\cC)}
       d_i^2\theta_i^{\pm1}\chi_i(a),
  \qquad a\in A_{\cC},
\]
where $\chi_i(a)$ is the scalar by which $a$ acts on $i$.  Neither this full
coefficient-group invariant nor its simply connected closed formulas are
claimed or Lean-verified here.
